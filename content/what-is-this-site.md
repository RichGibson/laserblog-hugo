"laserloveandbeer" is my personal site where I talk about my projects. It is not a business or a side hustle.

I'm Rich Gibson. I make things with lasers, tinker with hardware, write code, take photos, and occasionally make beer. This site is where I write up what I'm doing -- mostly for my own reference, sometimes because someone might find it useful.

There's no product, no newsletter, no funnel. With that said, I am underemployed and it would be fun
to work on something with other people.
